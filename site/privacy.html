Privacy

Last updated: [DATE]. Applies to the Kerf desktop app and this website.

Short version: Kerf edits your videos on your computer. We do not run accounts, analytics or ads, and your footage is never uploaded.

What stays on your device

Your videos, photos, audio, projects, captions and exports are processed and stored on your device. Kerf does not send them to us or to anyone else. Captions are generated on the device by a speech model that runs locally.

What goes online

When you first generate captions, the app downloads a speech model file from Hugging Face. That request is made by your computer directly to those services, so they see your IP address as they would for any download, under their own privacy policies. No audio, video or photo is sent. Once a model is on your device, that feature works offline.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting service in the app. This website is static files and does not use cookies or trackers.

Sharing your exports

When you share or export a video, you choose where it goes. What happens after that is up to the app or service you picked.

Children

Kerf is a general editing tool and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
