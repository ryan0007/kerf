Video editing for Windows, built for speed

Edit like it's instant.

Kerf is a native, GPU-accelerated video editor. Scrubbing, layers and effects stay smooth, with no account, no watermark and no ads.

Download for Windows

What's in the editor

Fast preview

The GPU draws every frame, so scrubbing and stacking layers stay smooth.

Captions

Speech to timed captions, transcribed on your computer, thirteen styles, per-caption position and speaker colours, SRT and VTT export.

Cut to the beat

Find the tempo of a music track, see the beats on the timeline, cut or fit your clips to them.

Remove silences

Find the quiet gaps in a clip and cut them all out in one step.

Layers and canvas

Overlays, masks, keyframes, speed ramps, text and stickers, and any canvas shape with a blurred or gradient background.

Timeline tools

Copy, paste and duplicate clips, right-click menus, markers for beats and scene changes, all with undo.

Download

An early preview of the Windows desktop app. It is free, needs no account, and is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen will warn you: choose "More info", then "Run anyway".

Latest Windows release

Unzip and run kerf-desktop.exe. Windows 10 or 11. Captions download a speech model (about 60 MB) the first time you use them. There is no installer or auto-update yet.
