A simple rule for pricing

If it runs on your phone, it costs us nothing, so it is free. If it runs on our servers, it costs real money, so that part is paid.

Free, on your device

  • The whole editor: timeline, layers, effects, transitions
  • Captions from speech, transcribed on the phone
  • Export up to the limit of your phone's encoder
  • No sign-in, no watermark, no ads

Paid, only if you want it

  • Cloud storage and sync of projects and media
  • Anything that needs server-side AI
  • If you stop paying, the app keeps working on your device and nothing local is touched

Plans and prices are not set yet.

What's in the editor

Fast preview

A Vulkan compositor draws every frame on the GPU, so scrubbing and stacking layers stay smooth.

Captions

Speech to timed captions, thirteen styles, per-caption position and speaker colours, SRT and VTT export.

Cut to the beat

Find the tempo of a music track, see the beats on the timeline, cut or fit your clips to them.

Remove silences

Find the quiet gaps in a clip and cut them all out in one step.

Layers and canvas

Overlays, masks, keyframes, speed ramps, text and stickers, and any canvas shape with a blurred or gradient background.

Multi-select

Copy and paste clips, select a range, trim or move a group together, all with undo.

Download

An early preview of the Windows desktop app. It is free, needs no account, and is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen will warn you: choose "More info", then "Run anyway".

Latest Windows release

Unzip and run kerf-desktop.exe. Windows 10 or 11. Captions download a speech model (about 60 MB) the first time you use them. There is no installer or auto-update yet.

Where it is

Kerf is in active development. It is being built for Android first, and an early preview of the Windows desktop app is available above. An iOS app is planned.

Many features are written and tested on a computer, and are still being checked on real phones. The Android app is not released yet; this page will say so when it is.
