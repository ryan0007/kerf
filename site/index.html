Features

Everything for a finished edit

The tools you reach for most, built into one fast editor.

Fast, smooth preview

The GPU draws every frame, so scrubbing and stacking layers stay responsive, even with 4K footage.

Layers and effects

Overlays, masks, keyframes, speed ramps, blend modes, green screen, 28 transitions, looks and canvas fills.

Captions from speech

Transcribed on your computer, in 16 languages. Thirteen styles, per-caption editing, SRT and VTT export.

Cut to the beat

Find the tempo of a music track, see beat markers on the timeline, and cut your clips to them.

Remove silences

Find the quiet gaps in a clip and cut them all out in one step.

A timeline that stays out of the way

Thumbnails and waveforms, free placement, copy, paste and duplicate, right-click menus, full undo.

Clean up your audio

Reduce steady noise or isolate a voice, normalise loudness, and browse a built-in sound library.

Export up to 4K

H.264 and AAC MP4 at 480p to 4K for any canvas shape, using your hardware encoder when you have one.

FAQ

Questions

Short answers to the usual ones.

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no charge, no watermark and no ads.

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign in to.

Does my footage get uploaded?

No. Editing, captions and export all happen on your computer. The only thing the app downloads is the speech model, the first time you use captions. See the privacy page.

Why does Windows warn me when I run it?

The preview is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen does not recognise it. Choose “More info”, then “Run anyway”.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once the caption model has been downloaded. Editing and export need no connection.

Is there a Mac or mobile version?

Not yet. This preview is Windows only.
